# Figma Make Prompt – Modern Multidisciplinary Designer Portfolio

## Objective

Design a premium, modern, highly interactive personal portfolio website for a multidisciplinary designer. The website should position the designer **not as someone who does many different kinds of design, but as someone who solves complex problems through the most appropriate medium.** Every design decision should reinforce strategic thinking, craftsmanship, and storytelling.

The visual language should feel like a blend of a high-end product design portfolio, an award-winning agency website, and a modern editorial experience. The site should be immersive without sacrificing usability or performance.

# Visual Style

Create a timeless, premium interface.

### Design Direction

* Editorial-inspired layouts
* Large typography
* Strong visual hierarchy
* Plenty of whitespace
* Clean geometric layouts
* Sophisticated grid system
* Soft rounded corners (12–20px)
* Elegant shadows only where necessary
* Premium image treatments
* Glassmorphism only in subtle elements
* Light theme by default with optional dark mode
* Smooth scrolling experience

The interface should feel handcrafted rather than template-based.

---

# Design Constraints

### Do

* Prioritize storytelling over decoration.
* Showcase quality over quantity.
* Use large typography and generous whitespace.
* Maintain a cohesive visual system.
* Keep interactions purposeful.
* Balance creativity with usability.
* Use immersive layouts that remain easy to navigate.
* Create a polished, high-end experience suitable for senior product, UX, and multidisciplinary design roles.

### Don't

* Do not use generic portfolio templates.
* Do not overload pages with animations.
* Do not use skill-rating bars or percentage indicators.
* Do not rely on neon colors or flashy gradients.
* Do not clutter the interface with excessive text.
* Do not use heavy shadows or skeuomorphic effects.
* Do not create disconnected sections; every section should contribute to a cohesive narrative.
* Do not sacrifice accessibility or responsiveness for visual flair.

---

# Creative Freedom

Treat this as a flagship portfolio worthy of recognition on platforms like Awwwards and CSS Design Awards. Explore bold editorial layouts, immersive scrolling, refined motion, and innovative interactions, but always keep clarity, usability, accessibility, and storytelling at the core of the experience. Every interaction should reinforce the narrative of a multidisciplinary designer who solves problems across products, brands, motion, and learning—not someone merely showcasing different categories of work.
